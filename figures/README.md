# Figures

`evaluate` generates ten PNG/SVG figure pairs in a run's `plots/` directory: training loss, Q intervals and zoom, outlet predictions, scatter, the combined record and four tree-importance charts.

These are reconstruction figures. The original paper figures cannot be regenerated from the available source and artifacts; see the [research audit](../docs/RESEARCH_AUDIT.md).

Generated measurement-derived images stay local and are ignored by Git. Sharing them depends on the data-use agreement.
