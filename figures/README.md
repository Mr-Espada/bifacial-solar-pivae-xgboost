# Figures and provenance

No original paper figure or raw-data-derived reconstruction image is redistributed here. The audit maps every published figure to available or missing evidence. Visual resemblance alone does not establish figure provenance or individual authorship.

With authorized private data and compatible fitted artifacts, `evaluate` generates ten PNG/SVG figure pairs under a private run's `plots/`: training loss, Q intervals/zoom, outlet predictions, scatter, combined record and four tree-gain charts. These are 2026 reconstruction plots and must not be captioned as independently regenerated original paper figures. New figure distribution is subject to the data-use agreement. Generated plots, like raw predictions and model weights, are ignored by Git.
