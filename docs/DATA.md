# Data access and exclusion

The publication attributes the five-second measurements to the Institute of New Energy Systems (InES), Germany. Consent to use them in this research is described in the paper; a grant to redistribute the CSVs is not present in the retained materials. No raw measurements, row-level predictions, trained weights, posterior draws, original notebook outputs, or data-derived time-series images are included in the candidate.

For local use, obtain authorized `train_simulation.csv` and `test_simulation.csv` and pass their directory with `--data-dir`. No automatic downloader, sample of real observations, or fabricated replacement data is provided. Synthetic test fixtures test program behavior only; they are not scientific results.

The training and test SHA-256 hashes, schema and counts are recorded in `RESEARCH_AUDIT.md`. Public `metrics.csv` has 12 aggregate model/split records and no timestamps or individual sensor observations. Confirm that the data-use agreement permits sharing these new aggregated reconstruction summaries before public release.

For a future data release, obtain InES/rightsholder permission covering redistribution, allowed derived artifacts, citation/acknowledgement, and applicable access terms. The article's open-access license does not establish those separate rights.
